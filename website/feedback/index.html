What to send.

            
Pick a type and the form adapts. All four reach the same inbox.

          

          
    
            
  • 
              
              
    Bug report
    
              
    Something failed, or did what you didn't expect. Steps and diagnostics help most.
    
              
            
    • 
            
  • 
              
              
    Suggestion
    
              
    A feature, a format or a workflow you'd like Keystore Studio to handle.
    
              
            
    • 
            
  • 
              
              
    Feedback
    
              
    Friction, rough edges, confusing wording, or places the app could feel smoother.
    
              
            
    • 
            
  • 
              
              
    Praise
    
              
    What's useful and worth keeping. It helps decide what not to change.

Bug reports

            
Before you report a bug.

            
A few details make the difference between a guess and a fix.

          

          
    
            
  1. 
              1
              
    
                Copy diagnostics. In Keystore Studio, open Settings → Data and choose Copy diagnostics.
                It copies the app version, platform, data folder and the names and aliases of open keystores, never
                passwords or key material. Paste it into your message.
              
    
            
    2. 
            
  2. 
              2
              
    Say what happened. What you did, what you expected, and what happened instead, with the exact error text.
    
            
    3. 
            
  3. 
              3
              
    Name the format. The keystore or file type (PKCS#12, JKS, BCFKS, PEM…) and the tool that made it, if you know.
    
            
    4. 
            
  4. 
              4
              
    Reproduce it with a test file. Make a throwaway keystore with keytool or openssl and say how you made it, instead of sending a real one.
    
            
    5. 
            
  5. 
              5
              
    
                Check what's known. The known limitations
                and existing issues
                may already cover it.
